# DrumPanel market and viability

DrumPanel is for the workshops that turn used oil drums into sheet for metal art, starting with the Haitian drum artists of Croix-des-Bouquets wherever they now work, and for the cooperatives, fair trade partners and vocational schools around them. It replaces a method that costs almost nothing in money (fire, chisel, body weight) but costs smoke, noise and injuries, so its value is in safety and sheet quality, not in saving money. Commercial alternatives each do one step and cost about as much as the whole bench set: a manual drum deheader alone lists at USD 3,299, and a 36 in slip roll at USD 995 is rated below the thinnest drum wall. An open design matters because the set must be built and mended by a town fabricator. Prices were checked on the dates given. Method: STANDARDS section 23.

The largest single line, the vapour check kit (USD 600), is in line with the market: a pumped-capable four-gas detector lists at USD 537 to 1,017 and a manual aspirator kit at USD 162 to 207 (Instrumart, checked 2026-10-25). A cooperative can share one detector across several workshops, but no drum is cut without it.

## Open route

A town fabricator with a welder and a lathe builds the set from the drawings, the build plan and the BOM; a tool shop makes the punches, dies and discs. Fair trade partners and cooperatives carry the design to new workshops, and vocational schools can build sets as training projects. CERN-OHL-S keeps changes open, and the parametric model lets a builder change the drum size or the panel layout without a new design.
